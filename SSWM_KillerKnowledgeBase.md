### 🏢 About the Company

### 🚛 Services

**Q: What types of transport do you offer?**
A: Road (our core), maritime, and air. For road specifically, we cover: standard cargo, refrigerated/cold chain, pharmaceutical, and industrial goods.

**Q: Do you handle perishable goods?**
A: Yes — this is one of our main specialties. We transport fruits, vegetables, fish, and other perishables daily from Morocco to all of Europe using refrigerated trucks with full temperature monitoring.

**Q: Can you transport pharmaceutical products?**
A: Yes. We are trained and certified for pharmaceutical transport. Important note: we only accept full truckloads (complets) in express mode for pharma cargo — no shared or groupage loads.

**Q: Do you offer industrial transport?**
A: Yes. We have a specialized fleet and dedicated drivers for industrial materials, with daily departures between Morocco and Europe.

**Q: Do you offer air or maritime transport?**
A: Yes, in addition to road transport, SSWM offers maritime and air freight options to meet all client needs.

**Q: Can you handle high-value goods?**
A: Yes. We offer dedicated transport for high added-value products with the appropriate security and traceability measures.

**Q: Do you offer Track & Trace?**
A: Yes. All our shipments are trackable in real time. Our fleet is 100% GPS geolocated and we use digital tools to give you live access to your cargo status.

**Q: Can you handle customs and transit procedures?**
A: Yes. SSWM offers international transit and customs clearance support, and our teams are knowledgeable about all administrative procedures at port crossings.

---

### 🌡️ Cold Chain & Refrigerated Transport

**Q: What is temperature-controlled transport?**
A: It's the transport of goods that must be kept at specific temperatures throughout the journey — refrigerated or frozen. SSWM uses trucks equipped with data loggers and thermographs to continuously monitor and record temperature.

**Q: Which products need temperature-controlled transport?**
A: Fresh produce (fruits, vegetables), fish and seafood, frozen goods, and pharmaceutical products all require temperature control.

**Q: How do you guarantee temperature integrity during transport?**
A: We use refrigerated vehicles with onboard data loggers (enregistreurs de températures) and thermographs. Temperature is recorded continuously, and our teams are always reachable to provide real-time updates.

**Q: Do you offer non-stop/express refrigerated transport?**
A: Yes. We offer express non-stop refrigerated transport with double crew (two drivers) to ensure your cargo arrives as fast as possible without breaks.

## 12. GLOSSARY

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Fret** | Freight / cargo to be transported |
| **Complet** | Full truckload — the entire truck is reserved for one client |
| **Frigorifique** | Refrigerated vehicle/transport |
| **Température dirigée** | Temperature-controlled transport (cold chain) |
| **Double équipage** | Two-driver crew enabling non-stop delivery |
| **Simple équipage** | One driver (standard delivery) |
| **Data logger** | Electronic device that records temperature throughout transit |
| **Thermographe** | Instrument that records temperature variations |
| **Bi-température** | Truck with two separate temperature zones |
| **Affrété / partenaire affrété** | Chartered / subcontracted partner vehicle |
| **Import / Export** | Inbound to Morocco / Outbound from Morocco |
| **Passage portuaire** | Port crossing (e.g. Tanger Med) |
| **Dédouanement** | Customs clearance |
| **Transit international** | Movement of goods across international borders |
| **Carte grise** | Vehicle registration document (used to count fleet) |
| **Express / Non-stop** | Transport without intermediate stops — fastest option |
